# GKV/PV contribution engine and guided setup — PR A/B

Rule snapshot: `gkv-pv-2026-reviewed-2026-09-09`. Research retrieved on **9 September 2026** using network requests to BMG, DRV, GKV-Spitzenverband, the GKV guidance hosted by vdek, and Gesetze im Internet. ## Automatic support contract

`scope.kind = standard-domestic-no-employment` is an explicit declaration that **all** these limits hold. It is not inferred from pension amounts or age:

- One person, domestic ordinary GKV/PV, no employment/self-employment, Krankengeld entitlement, household/spousal assessment, special minimum exemption or other special membership rule.
- Pension phase: selected KVdR, voluntary, or unknown. Ordinary domestic statutory pensions and recurring standard Betriebsrenten, entered gross before tax and insurance. Multiple streams aggregate by class. Pension status/eligibility is never inferred from whether pension income is positive.
- Bridge phase: selected voluntary/unknown cover, no pension streams. Pension-applicant membership, family insurance, unemployment/social-benefit arrangements and bridge pensions need whole-phase manual totals in this version.
- Voluntary rental/capital inputs are **already contribution-relevant monthly estimates**, including expenses and attribution. Rental means pre-tax assessable rental surplus, not net spendable rent or gross receipts without expense treatment. Capital means assessable income, not portfolio balance, total return or withdrawal amount. This module neither estimates it nor automatically subtracts a second expense allowance. Explicit zero is required when absent.
- Parenthood and child recognition have been established. Birth years alone cannot establish eligibility of adopted/step/foster children, proof timing, or overseas relationships. Unresolved recognition requires manual totals. Other surcharge exceptions (service/benefit arrangements), Beihilfe, agricultural insurance and foreign coverage are outside scope.

Any additional/unclassified income, PKV, foreign or special pension (including orphan pensions), lump-sum payout, mixed private/employer-funded contract, employment participation or unresolved rule requires `manual-required`, then a **manual input for the entire affected phase**. This is an implementation limit, not a declaration that every Nebenjob or every private pension is legally exceptional. Ordinary standalone private pensions may be excluded under KVdR after verified classification; this API intentionally has no general “excluded income” classifier. PR B must not label such receipts a standard Betriebsrente or capital basis merely to fit this engine. Narrow scope rather than guess.

Missing data yields `incomplete`; malformed/contradictory data yields `invalid`. Neither includes financial totals. These conditions do not automatically request manual insurance. Arrays `[]` confirm no pensions/recognised children; numeric `0` confirms zero. `null`, strings, negative bases and nonfinite values are invalid. The manual variant needs no automatic-rate/family inputs and rejects extra automatic fields. Manual KV/PV are own monthly totals **after all subsidies/participation**, replacing automatic totals; assessment and automatic subsidy details are unavailable, not zero estimates.

## Monthly and annual conventions

All monetary fields are monthly EUR in the same period's nominal units; percentages are decimal fractions. Statutory percentages stay fixed. `indexedContributionThresholds(factor)` scales the ceiling, minimum and occupational threshold with the caller's positive cumulative inflation factor. The published minimum EUR 1,318.33 is the base snapshot, not an independently re-derived number each year. Path-specific inflation belongs to the common simulation, not React or this module.

The approved **birth-year-only Jan 1 annual approximation** uses `calendarYear - birthYear` for the whole year. A child is under 25 iff that difference is below 25; it ages out at the **start of the year it turns 25**. A child born in the calculation year counts from Jan 1 in this approximation. Likewise, a childless insured person starts paying the surcharge in the year they turn 23, not while their annual age is below 23. These are approximations: law applies birthday-month timing, including the month of a child's 25th birthday and surcharge after the insured's 23rd-birthday month. No day/month accuracy is claimed. Parenthood is a separate permanent flag, never derived from the count of under-25 children. The complete recognised-child list may include deceased children whose hypothetical age remains relevant; future births must be introduced only in their year, not passed as already recognised children.

Rows use unrounded arithmetic for projection/ledger reconciliation. This is **not** insurer cent rounding per contribution component or paying agent (§9 Selbstzahler). BMG's rounded PV maximum EUR 209.26 can differ from this projection's 5812.50 × 3.6% = EUR 209.25; do not claim exact notices. Display rounding must not become a second financial calculation.

## Decisions and obligations for PR B

1. Keep the yearly ledger authoritative. Call this engine once per person and applicable phase/year segment from common simulation code; reuse the result for deterministic, capital-search and bootstrap paths. Cards/charts must read those ledger values.
2. Select phase explicitly from pension commencement, separately from stopping work. Prefill commencement from earliest statutory pension stream, retain independent editing and mismatch validation. Pass explicit status; unknown must visibly say conservative voluntary assumption, **not** a guaranteed worst case. This module does not resolve partial-year phase durations; the common ledger must weight the applicable monthly results consistently.
3. Collect gross pensions and pre-tax assessment estimates without reversing net amounts. Cashflow before insurance is supplied by the common ledger, including spendable income and other deductions, excluding KV/PV and DRV subsidy. Deduct `ownKvMonthly` and `ownPvMonthly` once. Participation is not extra income; voluntary subsidy already reduces own burden. Never add `capitalAssessmentMonthly` to cashflow.
4. Keep separate bridge/pension capital estimates; index them and all monetary thresholds with each simulation path. Gather all required inputs even if a high pension currently fills the ceiling. No capital estimator in PR A/B.
5. Map actual unsupported circumstances to the scope gate, and persist manual totals at **phase** scope. Do not combine per-stream manual amounts with capped automatic contributions. Explain lost automatic coverage and require both totals, including explicit zero where intended.
6. Disclose annual PV timing and projection rounding; keep permanent parenthood separate from the children list. Validate recognition rather than inferring it from birth years. Insurer rate is explicit; rate-change collection delays and notice precision are outside this snapshot.
7. PR B owns UI, app-only scenario reset notice, phase/missing-state integration, ledger annualisation, and cross-mode/browser/persistence tests. No activation or storage change is part of PR A.


## PR B integration decisions

- `insuranceSetupIssues` checks phase-wide income categories, ordinary pension confirmations, and declared insurance circumstances before normalization. Missing ordinary data blocks the forecast; an unsupported income or circumstance requires whole-phase manual own KV/PV after subsidies, including years before a later unsupported receipt. Ordinary KVdR rental income may remain net. Private pensions and employment remain outside this implementation's classification scope, not inherently contributory or legally exceptional.
- Explicit pension commencement is initially prefilled from the earliest statutory stream, is independently editable, and must match that stream. A commencement before the current age is valid for an already receiving pensioner. Contributions apply only to the modeled post-work phase; accumulation income/insurance is outside this calculator. Row start age determines phase and stream activation (inclusive start, exclusive end); there are no partial-year segments.
- `calculateRetirementIncomeForYear` supplies income, other deductions, insurance and available cash to each retirement row. All monetary inputs and thresholds use that row's cumulative path inflation. Manual capital bases are separate for bridge/pension and add no cash. Rental assessment is a separate pre-tax, expense-adjusted estimate; the income stream separately models cash and other deductions.
- Required-capital search reuses the authoritative path ledger's withdrawals and the shared funding step. Its existing convention is retained: selected deterministic retirement return, unless an explicit search return resolver is supplied; inflation and contribution cashflows follow the supplied path. It does not substitute sampled returns merely because the ledger came from a bootstrap path.
- Advanced overrides change total general KV, reduced KV or base PV, not independent own-burden rates. Zusatzbeitrag, DRV participation/subsidy and child discounts continue to derive from these totals. Base PV overrides are at least 1% so the maximum 1-point child discount cannot create a negative rate. These are user projection assumptions, not alternate legal rules.
- New scenarios have unanswered status, circumstance, Zusatzbeitrag, parenthood and pension classification fields. Voluntary/unknown phases require explicit capital estimates (including zero); pension phases require an explicit subsidy selection. Manual phases need neither automatic rates nor family/capital inputs. Malformed values in fields that become irrelevant are discarded; valid advanced assumptions survive mode changes.
- Persistence v13 discards only owned scenario keys v1–v12 and records a dismissible reset notice. It does not migrate the obsolete activation, per-stream overrides or combined insurance haircut. Unanswered but structurally valid new scenarios persist; unrelated storage and newer scenario keys remain untouched.
- The monthly breakdown uses the selected ledger row and offers first-bridge/first-pension navigation, contribution bases, thresholds, own contributions, DRV participation/subsidy and available income. Values are nominal monthly averages, display-rounded independently, with the unrounded yearly ledger authoritative.
